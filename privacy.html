Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what Base Layer does with your information when you use this website.

On this page

  1. Information we collect
  2. How we use your information
  3. Email and third parties
  4. How long we keep it
  5. Your choices
  6. Changes to this policy

Information we collect

When you contact us or order by email, we receive the details you write in that email, such as your name, email address, delivery address, contact number, and the items you want. We do not collect payment details on this website.

How we use your information

We use your details only to answer your questions, confirm your order, and arrange delivery. We do not sell your information.

Email and third parties

Our contact form and order buttons open your own email app. Your message travels through your email provider, which has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order and inquiry emails only as long as needed to complete your order and keep our records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing baselayerorders@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

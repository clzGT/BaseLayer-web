About Us

Base Layer makes simple tees that go under everything and with everything. We started with one idea: a good tee should be comfortable enough for every day and good-looking enough to wear on its own.

Our designs are small and friendly. A briefcase for the workday, an alarm clock for the always-on-time, and a plain tee for everything else.

We are based at 19 Cornel St., Makati City. Have a question? Contact us.

What we care about

  • Comfort first

    Soft, easy-fitting tees made for long days.

  • Simple design

    Clean graphics that do not shout.

  • Easy ordering

    Pick your tee, send us an email, and we reply to confirm.
